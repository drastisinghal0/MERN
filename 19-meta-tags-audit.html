Why Meta Tags Matter

  • Charset: Displays characters correctly.
  • Viewport: Makes pages mobile-friendly.
  • Description: Helps search engines understand the page.
  • Keywords: Gives page keywords.
  • Robots: Tells search engines whether to index the page.
  • Theme Color: Changes browser theme color on mobile.
  • Open Graph: Improves link previews on social media.
